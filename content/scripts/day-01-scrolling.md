# Day 01 — Kenapa Kita Sulit Berhenti Scrolling?

## Brief
- Pillar: Everyday Life / Digital Life
- Target duration: 32 seconds
- Goal: recognition + curiosity + thoughtful comments
- Main idea: feeds can present a continuing stream of new material; cues and curiosity may make it easy to keep scrolling. Avoid asserting that all viewers experience the same mechanism.

## Hook options
1. "Cuma mau cek satu notifikasi, tiba-tiba sudah 30 menit."
2. "Pernah buka HP sebentar, lalu lupa waktu?"
3. "Satu video selesai. Kenapa tangan kita langsung mencari video berikutnya?"

Recommended: option 1. It opens with a specific, familiar moment.

## Voice-over draft
"Cuma mau cek satu notifikasi, tiba-tiba sudah 30 menit. Awalnya buka HP sebentar. Lalu satu video muncul, disusul video lain. Konten baru, rasa ingin tahu, dan feed yang terus berlanjut membuat kita punya banyak alasan untuk tetap menggulir. Coba sadari kapan kamu mulai membuka aplikasi, lalu tentukan kapan waktunya berhenti. Kamu juga sering begini?"

## Scene breakdown
| Time | Voice over / text | Visual direction | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Cuma mau cek satu notifikasi… | Large kinetic headline, notification dot | Pop-in, slight overshoot |
| 4–9s | Tiba-tiba sudah 30 menit | Phone UI motif, clock hands move | Clock spin, scroll loop |
| 9–18s | Satu video, lalu video lain | Three content cards stack vertically | Staggered slide-up |
| 18–25s | Feed terus berlanjut | Endless vertical stream motif | Looping scroll, controlled speed |
| 25–29s | Sadari kapan mulai dan berhenti | Mascot points to a simple timer | Soft reveal |
| 29–32s | Kamu juga sering begini? | Clean end card | Fade and hold |

## On-screen text
- Cuma mau cek satu notifikasi…
- Tiba-tiba sudah 30 menit.
- Konten baru.
- Rasa ingin tahu.
- Feed terus berlanjut.
- Sadari kapan mulai. Tentukan kapan berhenti.

## Research notes
This is a general behavioral explainer. Before publishing, add reliable sources about infinite scroll/interface design and attention habits. Do not claim a single universal psychological explanation. Avoid presenting the 30-minute example as a measured statistic.

## CTA
"Kamu juga sering begini? Ceritakan pengalamanmu di komentar."
